Nostr, piece by piece

An open protocol with no company in the middle. These are its parts, and what Avestruz does with each.

The ostrich

Notes and other stuff

Nostr is short for “Notes and Other Stuff Transmitted by Relays”. You are a key pair, what you say is a note you sign, and relays pass notes along. Any client reads the same notes, so nobody owns your account or your audience.

Its people call themselves ostriches. Avestruz is the Portuguese for one.

NIP-01

The mast

Relays

A relay is a plain server that keeps notes and hands them on. Nobody has to share one: each account says where it writes and where it reads, and a client looks for people there — the outbox model.

Avestruz reads everyone from the relays they write to, shows what each relay says of itself and warns about what it asks for, and lets you edit your own lists. No relay is built in for search or media: those are yours to choose.

NIP-65 NIP-11

The lines

Notes

Everything on Nostr is an event: a little signed message with a kind. A text note, a reply, a repost, a reaction, a comment — each travels from relay to relay, and anyone can check who signed it.

Avestruz shows the notes of the people you follow, with or without replies, opens any of them as its whole conversation, shows quoted notes in place, and keeps what it has seen so the feed is there at once the next time.

NIP-10 NIP-18 NIP-22 NIP-25

The bunker

Keys

Your identity is a key. The public half (npub) is your name; the secret half (nsec) signs what you say, and there is no “forgot my password”. A remote signer, a bunker, can hold the secret and sign on request, so a client never sees it.

Sign in to Avestruz with an nsec, an npub to only read, or a bunker:// address. Whatever is secret is kept in the system keyring and nowhere else.

NIP-19 NIP-46

The lightning rod

Zaps

A zap is a Lightning payment pinned to a note: a few sats sent straight to its author, with a receipt the relays carry so everyone can see it landed.

Avestruz counts the sats under every note and shows who sent them. To zap, it asks the author's Lightning address for an invoice and shows it as a QR code for your wallet; it holds no money itself, and says when the zap went through.

NIP-57

The flower

Pictures and videos

Notes are text. Pictures and videos live on media servers, filed under their own hash, and a note links to them — Blossom, as the scheme is called. Lose a server and the same file can be found on another.

Avestruz sends what you attach to your own servers and has the others fetch a copy. Before a picture leaves, everything in the file that is not the picture — where and when it was taken, with what — is left out. Videos play right in the feed, YouTube links too.

Blossom NIP-92

The mailbox

Writing

To publish is to sign a note and give it to relays: your own, and the ones the people you mention read. It counts once a relay has taken it.

Avestruz has a composer for notes, replies and quotes. What you wrote and did not send is never lost: it is encrypted to yourself and kept as a draft by a relay Avestruz runs inside itself, which listens on no port and serves nobody else.

NIP-37 NIP-44

The screen

In the panel

Avestruz is a button in the COSMIC panel and a popup that holds the whole client, as tall as your screen has room for. A key of your choosing opens it from anywhere.

Inside, the keyboard does everything: j and k walk the notes, l opens a thread, r replies, z zaps, b bookmarks, ? lists the rest. Close it mid-sentence and it is all still there when you come back.

The telescope

Search

No relay has everything, so search is something particular relays offer, and your account lists the ones you trust to do it.

Avestruz asks those and no others, for people and for notes at once, and puts the people you follow first. Nothing you search for is kept. Paste an npub, a note's address or a name at a domain and it opens straight away.

NIP-50 NIP-05

The book

Bookmarks

Lists are notes too: who you follow, what you keep, which relays you use. Part of a list can be encrypted, so that only you can read it, wherever you sign in.

Avestruz keeps bookmarks that way, private, and has a page to browse them. Bookmarked notes are also never dropped from its local store.

NIP-51

A whole client in a popup

Native to the desktop, quick to open, quick to put away.

The feed

Notes from the people you follow, with or without replies. What was seen before shows at once; new notes wait behind a button, so the list never jumps under you.

Threads and quotes

Any note opens as its conversation. Quoted notes show in place, long notes fold, links get a preview card.

Writing, with drafts

Notes, replies and quotes, published to your relays and to those of the people you name. Leave whenever: the draft is kept, encrypted, across restarts.

Attachments

Pictures, videos and sounds from the file chooser or the clipboard, sent to your media servers and mirrored. Pictures go out with their metadata removed.

Videos in place

A video plays in its note through GStreamer, with sound, seeking and speed. YouTube links play there too, with yt-dlp — there is no web engine inside.

Reactions and zaps

React with any emoji, repost or quote, zap by QR code. A right click on a count shows who they were.

Notifications

Replies, mentions, quotes, reactions, reposts and zaps in one page, each with the note it is about.

Private bookmarks

Kept encrypted in your own list, so they follow your account and nobody else reads them.

Search

People and notes, on the search relays you chose. Names, npubs and note addresses open directly.

Your relays, your way

The outbox model throughout, a page for your relay lists with what each relay asks of you, and a look at any relay by its address.

Keyboard all the way

Move, open, reply, zap, bookmark, play and share without the mouse. One key opens the popup, another shows what every key does.

Where you left it

The popup closes on a click outside; nothing in it is lost. The feed, how far it was scrolled and whatever you were writing are there when it opens again.

Get it

Packages for Debian, Ubuntu and RPM distributions, a tarball for the rest, or build it from source.

From source

A Rust toolchain and just, plus what any libcosmic application needs and the GStreamer development packages.

git clone https://github.com/glima/avestruz.git
cd avestruz
just build-release && sudo just install

Dependencies per distribution are listed in the README.

Then, in COSMIC

Add Avestruz to the panel from Settings → Desktop → Panel → Applets. To open it with a key, bind one to:

cosmic-applet-avestruz --toggle

Videos play with the GStreamer plugins on your machine; YouTube links need yt-dlp.

Avestruz is young: expect it to change often.

Built with care

A client holds the key to who you are. It should deserve that.

Your key stays put

Secrets live in the system keyring, read for each signature and not held. With a bunker, the key never reaches Avestruz at all.

No open ports

Nothing listens on the network. The relay it runs for your drafts is reached through a pipe inside the program, and what is unpublished is encrypted on disk.

No servers of ours

There is no Avestruz service, account or telemetry. Search relays and media servers are the ones you set, and media servers must be HTTPS.

Checked continuously

Memory-safe Rust with strict lints, dependency licence and advisory audits, fuzzing of the parsers that read what strangers send, and checksums with every release.
